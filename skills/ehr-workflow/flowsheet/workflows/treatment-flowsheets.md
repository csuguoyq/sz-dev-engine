# Working with Treatment Flowsheets

> Companion to `skills/ehr-workflow/flowsheet/SKILL.md`. Read this file when your task involves **treatment flowsheets** — streamlined flowsheet workflows for nurses, providers, and ancillary staff such as respiratory care.
> Source: EHR Online Help `Working_with_Flowsheets/Working_with_Treatment_Flowsheets.htm`.

Treatment flowsheets **combine features of other flowsheets**, including:

- **Off-hour columns for respiratory medication orders** alongside **fixed-hour columns for respiratory treatment orders**.
- Orders from an **HL7 interface or Order Entry** display in **specifically labeled sections**.
- **Interfaced orders** can be configured to sort with **newest orders listed first** and oldest listed last.
- **Scheduled orders** may contain **schedule markers** like those described in the MAR flowsheet; these orders may have various frequencies, including **one-time (x1) treatments**.
- **One-time (x1) treatments** can be configured to allow users to select **Noted** or **Acknowledgment** options that **do not count** towards satisfying the frequency of administration.
- **Cells with edited or deleted entries** have a **black triangle** in the top-right corner of the cell, indicating modification or deletion ([symbols.md](symbols.md)).
