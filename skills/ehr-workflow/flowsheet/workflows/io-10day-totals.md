# Viewing I&O Up to 10-Day Totals

> Companion to `skills/ehr-workflow/flowsheet/SKILL.md`. Read this file when your task involves the **I&O Up to 10-Day Totals** dialog (intake/output totals snapshot from a flowsheet).
> Source: EHR Online Help `Working_with_Flowsheets/Viewing_IO_Up_to_10-Day_Totals.htm`. I&O charting itself is covered by the separate (non-converted) I&O help section; the I&O split pane is in [io-split-pane.md](io-split-pane.md).

To view the I&O Up to 10-Day Totals:

1. Select the **I&O Up to 10-Day Totals** icon on the flowsheet → the **I&O Up to 10-Day Totals dialog** displays:

- **Ten days of data** display, from the date the icon is selected.
- **Intake, Output, and Net** totals for **each date** and **intake/output type** are displayed.
- The **last weight** (in kg or gm) for each date displays **below the Net totals** if weight is recorded.
- **Averages** are based on the volume divided by the **number of days of charted data** for each volume type.
